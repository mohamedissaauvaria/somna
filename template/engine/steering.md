# Steering for the nightly cycle

Tell the cycle what matters to you and what to ignore. Edit freely.

## Pay attention to
- Corrections I made to an agent's output, especially repeated ones.
- Review comments on pull requests, documents and emails.
- Tool errors that happened more than once (build tools, CLIs, scripts).
- Preferences I stated about tone, format or structure.

## Ignore
- One-off typos and small talk.
- Anything only true for a single day, like today's meeting times.
- Facts already written correctly in memory.

## Thresholds
- A new rule needs evidence from at least 2 different sessions,
  unless I stated it directly ("from now on...", "always...", "never...").
